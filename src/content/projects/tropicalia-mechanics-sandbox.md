## Overview

Before committing to a full game loop, I use small isolated browser sandboxes to test whether a single core mechanic generates interesting decisions on a 6x6 grid.

## Experiments Included

- **Momentum vs. Terrain**: Units preserve directional velocity across turns, turning positioning into a spatial puzzle rather than simple range calculation.
- **Shared Action Economy**: Drawing actions from a common environmental pool that shifts based on weather and tempo.
